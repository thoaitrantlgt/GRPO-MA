# CPU gate research contract

Scope: fixed-policy independent Bernoulli continuations within a fixed thought group. Evaluate estimation, not online reward optimization. This contract is derived from the user-authorized CPU gate, not a new research direction.

- C1 (mathematical): precommitted raw pilot/main reuse is unbiased in thought values, with diagonal covariance under independent streams and pilot-measurable positive main counts. Verify exact small cases.
- C2 (mathematical): the analytic Jacobian and leverage column norms match normalized population-variance advantages. Greedy allocation is optimal only for the fixed separable local inverse-count surrogate.
- C3 (empirical hypothesis): plug-in JADA produces clear finite-budget normalized-advantage MSE improvement over variance-only on a mixture declared before new outcomes. This may fail; retain negative results. Compare identical reuse and count pilot costs.
- C4 (diagnostic): pilot fraction, group dispersion and leverage characterize where the estimator works or fails. These descriptive associations are not causal or LLM-generalization claims.

No CPU outcome establishes gradient improvement, online training gains, novelty or cross-task generalization. A surrogate oracle is not a nonlinear-MSE lower bound. Gate threshold and estimand are recorded in experiments/cpu_gate/protocol.json; K2 is an equality boundary.
